# Flowmo

Flowmo is an open-ended focus ritual in which the person chooses when Focus
ends and earns rest in proportion to the time focused.

## The loop

**Flowmodoro**:
An open-ended focus pattern where Focus counts up until the person stops and the
following Break is proportional to that Focus.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, fixed timer

**Loop**:
The product journey through Idle, Prime, Focus, Break, Reflection, Close Beat,
and back to Idle.

**Loop Beat**:
One named step within a Live Session: Prime, Focus, Break, Reflection, or Close
Beat.

**Session**:
One attempt centered on one Intention and moving through the Loop.

**Live Session**:
A Session that has started and has not been completed, canceled, or replaced. A
Recovery Pause remains part of the same Live Session.

**Completed Session**:
A Session accepted at its Close Beat and retained in History.

**Idle**:
The condition outside a Live Session where an Intention can be edited and
explicitly started.

**Intention**:
The one editable line chosen at Idle and carried into Prime. It is not entered
again during Prime.

**Prime**:
The settling step immediately before Focus, with the Intention still visible.

**Focus**:
The open-ended working step that counts upward and ends when the person chooses
to stop.
_Avoid_: Encoding

**Break**:
The self-directed rest step after Focus, with a recommended duration earned from
that Focus.

**Break Ratio**:
The stable rule relating focused time to the recommended Break.
_Avoid_: Learned ratio, adaptive ratio

**Reflection**:
The brief optional step after Break for choosing where to resume next.
_Avoid_: Recall as the user-facing name

**Close Beat**:
The final review before a Session becomes completed and Flowmo returns to Idle.
_Avoid_: Close when the full term prevents ambiguity

**Recovery Pause**:
A lifecycle-created frozen Live Session awaiting Continue or Restart. It is not
a voluntary Focus control.
_Avoid_: Pause

## Continuity

**Parked Thought**:
A one-line thought saved during Focus without ending it and retained with that
Session.
_Avoid_: Capture when naming the saved thought

**Next Step**:
An optional Reflection line describing where to resume. It may be carried or
restored into Idle but never starts automatically.
_Avoid_: Recall text, resumption intention, cue when this exact line is meant

**History**:
The ordered collection of Completed Sessions from which an Intention or Next
Step may be restored.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, thread

## Supporting behavior

**Focus Guard**:
Optional, fail-open friction for selected apps during unpaused Focus. It is not
a lock.

**Work Handoff**:
The optional return to the work app active immediately before Flowmo after the
person explicitly chooses Focus now.

**Focus Scene**:
A transient Mac presentation of Focus within the same Live Session. It is
neither a new Session nor a new Loop Beat.

**Glance**:
A read-only supporting view of the current clock that never controls the
Session.

**Session Conflict**:
Cross-device or account state that cannot be safely reconciled and therefore
requires an explicit choose-one decision.
