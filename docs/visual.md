# Flowmo — visual rules

Compact Flowmo is one charcoal square with one circular aperture that does not move. Phase is what the aperture holds. The Distant Horizon is the active iPhone Focus canvas and an optional Mac Focus presentation. Gold marks rest being earned or already earned, never a Focus finish line.

- **Field** `#090A0C` with a warm radial well. **Ink** `#F3F1EA`. Supporting copy uses readable warm-gray **mute** `#9A958A` and **faint** `#817D73` tokens.
- **Type.** SF Rounded on clock, captions, and fields. No serif. No italic as identity.
- **Introduction.** Three short, skippable pages in the existing frame precede the first safe Idle. Same charcoal field, rounded type, and gold details; no separate Home or setup surface. The pages explain the full loop, finish at **Set intention**, and offer optional **Enable notifications**. Data exposes **How it works** for replay. The layout scrolls when necessary; Reduce Motion disables page-transition animation. Mac temporarily presents it in Classic and preserves Mini as the saved preference. Behavior stays in [`PROJECT.md`](PROJECT.md#first-use-tutorial).
- **Aperture.** In compact presentations, the fixed circle is matched across phases. It has a 5pt track, inner highlight, and gold glow on Break. The hole is a lunar face: surface turns, light stays. Maria stay charcoal. Idle breathes unless Reduce Motion. Reduce Motion freezes the face. Active iPhone Focus uses Distant Horizon instead; Recovery Pause returns to the aperture.
- **Grid.** Compact phases use the same slots: 44pt caption, aperture, verb (36 Mac / 44 phone), then reserved idle chrome (Today / History / Data / New, Guard on Mac). Live compact phases keep that chrome empty so Start → Prime does not grow the hole into the verbs. Start, Focus now, Reflect, Skip / Done, and Close Beat Done sit on the same baseline. On iPhone, accessibility text sizes or a short available height switch compact beats to a scrolling column with flexible caption/action heights. The active iPhone Focus canvas follows the separate Focus Scene layout below.
- **Idle.** Field placeholder: **Intention**. The box starts empty unless this surface just completed a Close Beat with an explicit next step; then that exact trimmed step is already visible and editable, while **Start** remains separate. A truly new store puts three short lines inside the aperture: **Focus counts up. Stop when you’re ready. Your break grows as you focus.** When no draft is visible and the latest completed session has a next step, **Use next** reveals it for confirmation; it never falls back to an older session. Otherwise **Use last** can reveal the saved intention. In Mini a carried step or either reveal expands to Classic. **Start** begins only visible text. **New** clears only a line you have not started. Footer: Today, History, Data, New. Tap the Mini tile to expand and type.
- **History.** Same well cards. Collapsed: intention, date in mute, focus clock, and a count if there is writing. Tap opens that card only. Intention wraps. **Focused** stays ink. **Break earned** is gold. **Parked** and **Next step** appear only when they exist. Expanded cards offer **Use next step** or **Use intention** as the fallback; either returns to Idle with the line visible and never auto-starts, and asks before replacing a typed draft. Tap again, or tap another, to close. Height eases 0.28s. No sheet.
- **Phone readability.** The first-session reminder uses wrapping semantic text. Idle's History, Data, and New actions have at least 44×44pt touch targets; Today moves above their row when horizontal room is insufficient. Data and the tutorial scroll instead of compressing their controls. These are adaptations of the existing states, not new screens or navigation.
- **Prime.** Intention in the caption, cue **Prepare**. Clock in a graphite ring. **Focus now** makes the immediate path explicit. Mac also offers one ink **Focus scene** action: it starts the same Focus in the large, movable Distant Horizon canvas. Mac automatic expiry never switches apps or activates a Scene. On iPhone, either Prime exit opens the phone-native Distant Horizon because the person is already inside the one phone frame.
- **Focus.** No ring. Mac Classic keeps the intention, **Focus**, count-up, and gold accrual in the fixed aperture; Mini keeps the compact clock and `+`. On iPhone, active unpaused Focus uses the edge-to-edge Distant Horizon with the intention, gold count-up, earned-rest mark, **Park thought**, and **End focus**. Capture remains on the canvas and replaces those actions with **Discard** / **Park**. Recovery Pause uses the frozen compact aperture on every platform.
- **Break.** Neutral caption: **Break**, with the cue **Take what you need.** Same ring geometry; progress is gold. Beneath the countdown, state the earned rest and source Focus duration in gold (for example, **10m earned from 50m focus**). **Reflect** advances early.
- **Reflection — 3:00.** Prompt: **Where will you pick up next?** Writing lives in the caption slot (same field as Intention), not inside the circle. Clock fills the hole like Prime. The verb is **Skip** while empty and **Done** once written. If thoughts were parked and the field is empty, **Review N parked** keeps the timed ring, shows one line at a time newest-first inside the aperture, and offers **Use as next**; the selected line returns to the editable field and remains in History. Return ends the beat.
- **Close Beat.** Intention in the caption. The hole shows **Focused**, **Break earned**, and—when present—the Next Step and Parked Thought count. A visible **Done** records the Session and carries that exact Next Step into editable Idle without starting; with no Next Step, Idle stays empty. No score and no receipt.
- **Recovery Pause.** Aperture frozen. **Restart** and **Continue** occupy the verb slot. Restart primes the same Intention again. Continue resumes the frozen clocks. No Paused banner.
- **Chrome.** Hidden title, traffic lights. Classic: mute, pin, and one presentation control on the right. Mini: mute + pin on the left (clear of the traffic lights), presentation control on the right. Outside Focus it is the quiet Classic/Mini menu. During an unpaused Focus it becomes one visible sun-and-horizon primary action that enters Scene directly. Its adjacent disclosure repeats **Focus Scene** before Classic/Mini, so either side of the control provides the return path; a menu choice dismisses before the window morphs. The live Focus verb row also exposes a named **Focus scene** action in Classic and **Scene** in Mini, so returning never depends on recognizing chrome. Mute and pin keep Focus opacity 0.35 on Classic; the Scene entry remains visible. Mini chrome stays at 1. The wall does not fade.
- **Buttons.** Start, Prime's Mac **Focus scene**, and recovery **Continue**: an ink chip that brightens and glows on hover. **Focus now**, Reflect, Skip / Done, live-Focus **Focus scene** / **Scene**, Park thought, Stop, History, Guard, and New: mute until hover — ink, a capsule well, and a 1pt ink stroke around the capsule. In the Mac Scene, **End focus** is gold and **Back to window** is ink; both gain the same quiet capsule hover and visible keyboard focus. The phone horizon keeps 44pt touch targets, with **End focus** in gold and Park actions in ink. No underline. Chrome icons: ink + circular well. No grow-as-affordance.
- **Clock.** SF Rounded `.medium`, tabular. Timed ~36–40pt; compact Focus ~52–56pt; phone Distant Horizon Focus scales responsively from about 27–42pt.
- **Motion.** Phase: the circle stays put. Ring and hole assemble in place — four arcs click together, contents implode, nothing flies off or slides into the verbs. Captions and verbs ease (0.28s). Clock: numericText. Ring trim eases. No wallpaper fade. Lunar face turns in 96s (light fixed). Focus count grows once at **5 / 10 / 15 / 30 / 45 / 60**. Break last **10s** pulse each second (gold glow only — the circle does not move). Reduce Motion fades the assemble and skips grow, race, and the turn.

**Focus Scene.** A charcoal canvas with a thin full-width gold horizon arcing through the lower middle. It is ambient geometry, never progress. The header begins near the leading edge and upper tenth: mute **Focus scene**, the intention in responsive SF Rounded, then a gold tabular count-up. The native horizon breathes almost imperceptibly over 12 seconds; Reduce Motion keeps it static. It owns no new font, raster asset, data, ring, deadline, or session state.

On Mac, the canvas is a large borderless window on one display. It starts centered at about 88% of the display width and 86% of its visible height, capped at 1440×900 with a 720×480 minimum. Drag the empty canvas to move it; resize from any edge. Screen changes keep the one Scene clamped to the visible display. **End focus** and **Back to window** sit at the lower corners. Back restores the normal window without changing apps or the exact Focus; the normal window exposes both the named Focus verb and the direct sun-and-horizon entry again. A pending Focus Guard decision replaces both exit actions.

On iPhone, the canvas fills the one safe-area-aware app frame in portrait or landscape whenever Focus is active and unpaused. It retains the earned-rest mark, mute, and touch-native **Park thought** / **End focus** controls; capture stays on the canvas. Larger accessibility sizes stack the actions instead of shrinking their text, and constrained layouts scroll so controls remain reachable above the keyboard. There is no movable-window, Back, pin, app-switch, or Focus Guard behavior. Recovery Pause returns to the ordinary compact aperture.

**Mac compact modes.** Classic and Mini have two fixed sizes; only the transient
Mac Focus Scene resizes freely.

- **Classic — 320×460.** The full instrument: caption, aperture, verb, Idle chrome (Today, History, Data, Guard). Fields are `FlowField`: delayed autofocus; a single hairline marks the slot, and it brightens and blooms on focus. No key legend. Intention Return performs the visible Idle action; capture **Park** / **Discard** (Return / Esc still work); Reflection Return completes (text is saved as you type).
- **Mini — 168×176.** Aperture owns the tile. Clock (and gold accrual on Focus) in the hole; verb on the lower arc. Mute and pin sit left; the presentation control sits right. No fields. Typing (Intention, capture, Reflection) expands to Classic. Tap the Mini tile on Idle or Reflection to expand. Prime exposes Focus scene as one compact icon beside Focus now. During Focus the named **Scene** verb and the sun-and-horizon primary action enter Scene; its disclosure changes Classic/Mini. `+` in Mini Focus opens capture in Classic; the parked-review icon opens the Reflection chooser in Classic. Focus Scene temporarily replaces either saved mode without overwriting it.

Shared tokens and tutorial presentation live in `FlowmoLook` (`Atmosphere.canvas`, `DistantHorizonBackdrop`, `IntroductionView`, `Aperture`, `ApertureRing`, `PhaseColumn`, `InstrumentClock`, `Accrual`, `InkButton`, `QuietButton`, `RecoveryVerbs`). Mac fields live in `FlowField`. The widget uses the shared field and ink; Mac menu-bar legibility follows [`menu-bar.md`](menu-bar.md).

Out: theme packs, glass as identity, Focus progress ring, uppercase kickers as a system, serif inside the hole.
