# Flowmo — visual rules

Compact Flowmo is one charcoal square with one circular aperture that does not move. Phase is what the aperture holds. The Distant Horizon is the active iPhone Focus canvas and an optional Mac Focus presentation; its dawn is the one picture of the session shared by every surface. Champagne marks rest being earned or already earned, never a Focus finish line. The current visual polish was introduced in build 5 and is retained in build 6; the live horizon and glass controls are unreleased work after build 7; earlier preview artifacts remain unchanged.

- **Palette.** Graphite **field** `#090A0C`, ivory **ink** `#F3F1EA`, readable neutral **mute** `#AAA9A4` and **faint** `#898A87`, **line** `#1C1E21`, and **track** `#34373A`. The restrained warm-metal rest accent is **champagne** `#D6B889`.
- **Icon.** The integrated original aperture-and-incision icon uses graphite, an ivory rim, and a restrained champagne opening. It is a provisional visual identity; the working product name remains Flowmo. The current shared Mac/iPhone catalog uses its size derivatives; the earlier crescent master remains as build 4 provenance. Source and generation records are in [`Apps/IconSource/README.md`](../Apps/IconSource/README.md).
- **Type.** Standard SF on clocks, captions, controls, and fields. Semantic text styles handle supporting copy; clocks use tabular digits. No added font, serif, or italic identity.
- **Introduction.** Three illustrated, skippable pages in the existing frame precede the first safe Idle: **Make room for one thing.**, **Stay with the work.**, and **Leave space to return.** A three-part indicator identifies the current step. Shared Horizon Study artwork frames illustrative clock/rest examples; it never reads or starts a session. The pages explain the full loop, finish at **Set intention**, and offer optional **Enable notifications**. Data exposes **How it works** for replay. The content scrolls, the artwork becomes shorter at accessibility text sizes, and iPhone footer actions stack when text needs more room; Reduce Motion disables page-transition animation. Mac temporarily presents it in Classic and preserves Mini as the saved preference. Behavior stays in [`PROJECT.md`](PROJECT.md#first-use-tutorial).
- **Aperture.** In compact presentations, the fixed circle is matched across phases. A static graphite instrument well has a continuous 2pt track; timed progress uses ivory, or champagne during Break. The well's lower rim carries the session's **dawn**: the same light the Distant Horizon shows, seen from above. It reaches into the field as a soft pool, opens over Prime, stays full through Focus while warming toward champagne with earned rest, closes over Break, and settles over Reflection. It is concentric on the aperture and never a fill; the circle itself never moves, scales, or rotates. There is no faux lunar surface, segmented puzzle assembly, or glowing progress ring. Active iPhone Focus uses Distant Horizon instead; Recovery Pause freezes the aperture and its dawn. Focus has no determinate ring.
- **Grid.** Compact phases use the same slots: 44pt caption, aperture, verb (36 Mac / at least 44 phone), then reserved idle chrome (Today / History / Data / New, Guard on Mac). Live compact phases keep that chrome empty so Start → Prime does not grow the hole into the verbs. Start, Focus now, Reflect, Skip / Done, and Close Beat Done sit on the same baseline. On iPhone, action height expands when labels or stacked buttons need room. Accessibility text sizes or a short available height also allow the caption to grow in the scrolling column. The active iPhone Focus canvas follows the separate Focus Scene layout below.
- **Guard panel.** Opening **Guard** from Idle lists the guarded apps in one quiet well card (the same treatment as History cards): one row per app with a hairline between rows, **Remove** at the trailing edge of each row, and **Add app** in the card's footer. Up to three rows show before the list scrolls; the aperture yields height to the card so it never runs off the fixed window.
- **Idle.** Field placeholder: **Intention**. The box starts empty unless this surface just completed a Close Beat with an explicit next step; then that exact trimmed step is already visible and editable, while **Start** remains separate. A truly new store puts three short lines inside the aperture: **Focus counts up. Stop when you’re ready. Your break grows as you focus.** When no draft is visible and the latest completed session has a next step, **Use next** reveals it for confirmation; it never falls back to an older session. Otherwise **Use last** can reveal the saved intention. In Mini a carried step or either reveal expands to Classic. **Start** begins only visible text. **New** clears only a line you have not started. Footer: Today, History, Data, New. Tap the Mini tile to expand and type.
- **History.** Same well cards. Collapsed: intention, date in mute, focus clock, and a count if there is writing. Tap opens that card only. Intention wraps. **Focused** stays ink. **Break earned** uses the rest accent. **Parked** and **Next step** appear only when they exist. Expanded cards offer **Use next step** or **Use intention** as the fallback; either returns to Idle with the line visible and never auto-starts, and asks before replacing a typed draft. Tap again, or tap another, to close. Height eases 0.24s. No sheet.
- **Phone readability.** The first-session reminder and phase cues use wrapping semantic text. Idle's History, Data, and New actions have at least 44×44pt touch targets; Today moves above their row when horizontal room is insufficient. Reflection actions use a vertical arrangement at accessibility sizes or when their row does not fit. Data, the tutorial, store recovery, unavailable-data, and sync-conflict content scroll instead of compressing their controls. Capture keeps its text field in the same hierarchy and allows its height to grow above the keyboard. These are adaptations of the existing states, not new screens or navigation.
- **Prime.** Intention in the caption, cue **Prepare**. Clock in a graphite ring. **Focus now** makes the immediate path explicit. Mac also offers one ink **Focus scene** action: it starts the same Focus in the large, movable Distant Horizon canvas. Mac automatic expiry never switches apps or activates a Scene. On iPhone, either Prime exit opens the phone-native Distant Horizon because the person is already inside the one phone frame.
- **Focus.** No ring. Mac Classic keeps the intention, **Focus**, count-up, and gold accrual in the fixed aperture; Mini keeps the compact clock and `+`. On iPhone, active unpaused Focus uses the edge-to-edge Distant Horizon with the intention, steady ivory count-up, champagne earned-rest mark, **Park thought**, and **End focus**. Capture remains on the canvas and replaces those actions with **Discard** / **Park**. Recovery Pause uses the frozen compact aperture on every platform.
- **Break.** Neutral caption: **Break**, with the cue **Take what you need.** Same ring geometry; progress is champagne. Beneath the countdown, state the earned rest and source Focus duration in champagne (for example, **10m earned from 50m focus**). **Reflect** advances early.
- **Reflection — 3:00.** Prompt: **Where will you pick up next?** Writing lives in the caption slot (same field as Intention), not inside the circle. Clock fills the hole like Prime. The verb is **Skip** while empty and **Done** once written. If thoughts were parked and the field is empty, **Review N parked** keeps the timed ring, shows one line at a time newest-first inside the aperture, and offers **Use as next**; the selected line returns to the editable field and remains in History. Return ends the beat.
- **Close Beat.** Intention in the caption. The hole shows **Focused**, **Break earned**, and—when present—the Next Step and Parked Thought count. A visible **Done** records the Session and carries that exact Next Step into editable Idle without starting; with no Next Step, Idle stays empty. No score and no receipt.
- **Recovery Pause.** Aperture frozen. **Restart** and **Continue** occupy the verb slot. Restart primes the same Intention again. Continue resumes the frozen clocks. No Paused banner.
- **Chrome.** Hidden title, traffic lights. Classic: mute, pin, and one Classic/Mini size menu on the right. Mini: mute + pin on the left (clear of the traffic lights), size menu on the right. On macOS 26 and later each chrome group sits in one Liquid Glass capsule; earlier systems show the bare glyphs. The live Focus verb row exposes the sole **Focus scene** action in Classic and **Scene** in Mini. Mute, pin, and size chrome keep Focus opacity 0.35 on Classic; Mini chrome stays at 1. The wall does not fade.
- **Buttons.** Start, Prime's Mac **Focus scene**, and recovery **Continue** use crisp ivory rounded rectangles on every system: 14pt corners, or 10pt for compact primary controls. A companion window is inactive most of the day and system glass dims with it, so the primary action never uses glass. Quiet actions, Scene actions, and phone Focus actions use Liquid Glass on macOS 26 / iOS 26 and later (12pt or 14pt continuous corners), following Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast; earlier systems keep the restrained 12pt well or the subtle fill and outline. Glass is for controls and chrome only, never the field or the horizon. Shared controls have at least 44pt height on iPhone, 36pt on Mac, or 28pt in compact Mac mode. Phone Focus actions have at least 48pt height. **End focus** uses the rest accent and Park actions use ink; Mac **Back to window** stays ink with visible keyboard focus. Press and hover change tone, without button halos, growth, or underlines. Chrome remains quiet and recognizable.
- **Clock.** Standard SF `.medium`, tabular, and ivory during Focus. Timed clocks are about 36–40pt; compact Focus is about 52–56pt. Phone Distant Horizon puts a calmer intention above a more prominent responsive clock, roughly 46–64pt with larger accessibility sizing. The earned-rest mark remains champagne. Long clocks fit the available width.
- **Motion.** The aperture and clocks keep their size and position. Instrument content settles with a short 0.18s fade; captions and verbs ease over 0.24s, and timed ring trim uses a short linear transition. The dawn's light eases over 0.6s when a phase changes, so it rises or sets rather than snapping. Clock digits use numeric transitions without milestone growth or a final-ten-seconds pulse. There is no puzzle assembly, implosion, or idle scaling, and the compact aperture never rotates. Entering the Mac Scene fades and rises the canvas over 0.6s; leaving it fades. Reduce Motion removes these optional transitions. The Distant Horizon has its own ambient treatment below.

**Focus Scene — the Distant Horizon.** A graphite canvas draws a vast dark planet below a limb of dawn light, rendered live in `FlowmoLook` from a Metal shader compiled at first use from source (`HorizonShader.swift`); no image, font, network request, or runtime service is involved, and where Metal is unavailable the bundled **Horizon Study** artwork remains as the fallback. The picture is a pure projection of `SessionStatus` (`HorizonState`), never a second clock:

- **Idle / Close Beat** — a hairline of pre-dawn at the horizon; sparse fixed stars.
- **Prime** — the dawn gathers at the horizon over Prime's determinate progress.
- **Focus** — full dawn. The planet's obsidian surface turns with focused time (30° per hour, unbounded, no target), and the light widens along the limb and warms from ivory toward champagne as rest is earned (asymptotic; never a fill). Stars fade where the dawn brightens.
- **Break** — the dawn sets over the countdown and the champagne drains with it.
- **Reflection** — dims back to the hairline.
- **Recovery Pause** — frozen at its exact values, with no ambient motion.

Ambient motion is decorative only: fine grain, a slow shimmer along the limb, and per-star brightness drift at 15 frames per second while the scene is active and Reduce Motion is off; otherwise the canvas draws once and rests. Sparse stars (about 80 on the Mac canvas, none in the compact frames) never twinkle. The header begins near the leading edge and upper tenth: a quiet phase label, the intention in responsive standard SF, an ivory tabular count-up, then the champagne earned-rest mark, exactly as on the phone canvas; a top scrim keeps it legible. The horizon itself introduces no session data, text, number, ring, deadline, or finish line. The numbers behind it live in `HorizonTuning`; source records live in [`PROVENANCE.md`](../PROVENANCE.md).

On Mac, the canvas is a large borderless window on one display. It starts centered at about 88% of the display width and 86% of its visible height, capped at 1440×900 with a 720×480 minimum. Drag the empty canvas to move it; resize from any edge. Display configuration changes keep the one Scene clamped to the visible display; crossing displays during a native drag does not reposition it. AppKit owns its size limits, so the canvas cannot inherit Classic/Mini constraints. **End focus** and **Back to window** sit at the lower corners. Back restores the normal window without changing apps or the exact Focus; the normal window exposes the named Focus verb again. A pending Focus Guard decision replaces both exit actions.

On iPhone, the canvas fills the one safe-area-aware portrait app frame whenever Focus is active and unpaused. The current phone targets support portrait only; constrained-height layouts remain usable with the keyboard. It retains the earned-rest mark, mute, and touch-native **Park thought** / **End focus** controls; capture stays on the canvas. Larger accessibility sizes stack the actions instead of shrinking their text, and constrained layouts scroll so controls remain reachable above the keyboard. There is no movable-window, Back, pin, app-switch, or Focus Guard behavior. Recovery Pause returns to the ordinary compact aperture.

**Mac compact modes.** Classic and Mini have two fixed sizes; only the transient
Mac Focus Scene resizes freely.

- **Classic — 320×460.** The full instrument: caption, aperture, verb, Idle chrome (Today, History, Data, Guard). Fields are `FlowField`: delayed autofocus; a single hairline marks the slot and brightens on focus without a halo. No key legend. Intention Return performs the visible Idle action; capture **Park** / **Discard** (Return / Esc still work); Reflection Return completes (text is saved as you type).
- **Mini — 168×176.** Aperture owns the tile. Clock (and gold accrual on Focus) in the hole; verb on the lower arc. Mute and pin sit left; the presentation control sits right. No fields. Typing (Intention, capture, Reflection) expands to Classic. Tap the Mini tile on Idle or Reflection to expand. Prime exposes Focus scene as one compact icon beside Focus now. During Focus the named **Scene** verb enters Scene; the chrome menu only changes Classic/Mini. `+` in Mini Focus opens capture in Classic; the parked-review icon opens the Reflection chooser in Classic. Focus Scene temporarily replaces either saved mode without overwriting it.

Shared tokens and tutorial presentation live in `FlowmoLook` (`Atmosphere.canvas`, `HorizonState` and `HorizonTuning`, `DistantHorizonBackdrop`, `HorizonSceneView`, the `\.horizon` environment value read by `Aperture`, `IntroductionView`, `Aperture`, `ApertureRing`, `PhaseColumn`, `InstrumentClock`, `Accrual`, `InkButton`, `QuietButton`, `RecoveryVerbs`, and the `instrumentSurface` / `chromeSurface` glass helpers). Mac fields live in `FlowField`. The widget uses the shared field and ink; Mac menu-bar legibility follows [`menu-bar.md`](menu-bar.md).

Out: theme packs, glass as identity (glass is for controls and chrome only), Focus progress ring or any Focus finish line, scheduled background events, twinkling stars, uppercase kickers as a system, serif inside the hole.
