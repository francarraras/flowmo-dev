# Flowmo — visual rules

One charcoal square. One circular aperture that does not move. Phase is what the aperture holds. Gold marks rest being earned or already earned, never a Focus finish line.

- **Field** `#090A0C` with a warm radial well. **Ink** `#F3F1EA`. Mute/faint stay of that ink.
- **Type.** SF Rounded on clock, captions, and fields. No serif. No italic as identity.
- **Aperture.** Fixed circle, matched across phases. 5pt track, inner highlight, gold glow on Break. The hole is a lunar face: surface turns, light stays. Maria stay charcoal. Idle breathes unless Reduce Motion. Reduce Motion freezes the face.
- **Grid.** Every phase uses the same slots: 44pt caption, aperture, verb (36 Mac / 44 phone), then reserved idle chrome (Today / History / New, Guard on Mac). Live phases keep that chrome empty so Start → Prime does not grow the hole into the verbs. Start, Focus now, Reflect, Skip / Done, and Close Beat Done sit on the same baseline.
- **Idle.** Field placeholder: **Intention**. The box starts empty unless this surface just completed a Close Beat with an explicit next step; then that exact trimmed step is already visible and editable, while **Start** remains separate. A truly new store puts three short lines inside the aperture: **Focus counts up. Stop when you’re ready. Your break grows as you focus.** When no draft is visible and the latest completed session has a next step, **Use next** reveals it for confirmation; it never falls back to an older session. Otherwise **Use last** can reveal the saved intention. In Mini a carried step or either reveal expands to Classic. **Start** begins only visible text. **New** clears only a line you have not started. Footer: Today, History, Data, New. Tap the Mini tile to expand and type.
- **History.** Same well cards. Collapsed: intention, date in mute, focus clock, and a count if there is writing. Tap opens that card only. Intention wraps. **Focused** stays ink. **Break earned** is gold. **Parked** and **Next step** appear only when they exist. Expanded cards offer **Use next step** or **Use intention** as the fallback; either returns to Idle with the line visible and never auto-starts, and asks before replacing a typed draft. Tap again, or tap another, to close. Height eases 0.28s. No sheet.
- **Prime.** Intention in the caption, cue **Prepare**. Clock in a graphite ring. **Focus now** makes the immediate path explicit. Mac also offers one ink **Focus scene** action: it starts the same Focus in the large, movable Distant Horizon canvas. Automatic expiry never switches apps or activates a Scene. Help and accessibility hints state the difference without a setup screen.
- **Focus.** Intention in the caption, cue **Focus**. No ring. Numerals fill the hole. Gold accrual with **no end cap**. **Park thought** and Stop share the verb slot; after a capture it becomes **Park another · N**. Capture replaces that row with **Park** / **Discard**. Mini retains `+` for space.
- **Break.** Neutral caption: **Break**, with the cue **Take what you need.** Same ring geometry; progress is gold. Beneath the countdown, state the earned rest and source Focus duration in gold (for example, **10m earned from 50m focus**). **Reflect** advances early.
- **Reflection — 3:00.** Prompt: **Where will you pick up next?** Writing lives in the caption slot (same field as Intention), not inside the circle. Clock fills the hole like Prime. The verb is **Skip** while empty and **Done** once written. If thoughts were parked and the field is empty, **Review N parked** keeps the timed ring, shows one line at a time newest-first inside the aperture, and offers **Use as next**; the selected line returns to the editable field and remains in History. Return ends the beat.
- **Close Beat.** Intention in the caption. The hole shows **Focused**, **Break earned**, and—when present—the Next Step and Parked Thought count. A visible **Done** records the Session and carries that exact Next Step into editable Idle without starting; with no Next Step, Idle stays empty. No score and no receipt.
- **Recovery Pause.** Aperture frozen. **Restart** and **Continue** occupy the verb slot. Restart primes the same Intention again. Continue resumes the frozen clocks. No Paused banner.
- **Chrome.** Hidden title, traffic lights. Classic: mute, pin, and one presentation control on the right. Mini: mute + pin on the left (clear of the traffic lights), presentation control on the right. Outside Focus it is the quiet Classic/Mini menu. During an unpaused Focus it becomes one visible sun-and-horizon primary action that enters Scene directly. Its adjacent disclosure repeats **Focus Scene** before Classic/Mini, so either side of the control provides the return path; a menu choice dismisses before the window morphs. Mute and pin keep Focus opacity 0.35 on Classic; the Scene entry remains visible. Mini chrome stays at 1. The wall does not fade.
- **Buttons.** Start, Mac Focus scene, and recovery Continue: an ink chip that brightens and glows on hover. Focus now, Reflect, Skip / Done, Park thought, Stop, History, Guard, and New: mute until hover — ink, a capsule well, and a 1pt ink stroke around the capsule. In the Scene, **End focus** is gold and **Back to window** is ink; both gain the same quiet capsule hover and visible keyboard focus. No underline. Chrome icons: ink + circular well. No grow-as-affordance.
- **Clock.** SF Rounded `.medium`, tabular. Timed ~36–40pt; Focus ~52–56pt.
- **Motion.** Phase: the circle stays put. Ring and hole assemble in place — four arcs click together, contents implode, nothing flies off or slides into the verbs. Captions and verbs ease (0.28s). Clock: numericText. Ring trim eases. No wallpaper fade. Lunar face turns in 96s (light fixed). Focus count grows once at **5 / 10 / 15 / 30 / 45 / 60**. Break last **10s** pulse each second (gold glow only — the circle does not move). Reduce Motion fades the assemble and skips grow, race, and the turn.

**Focus Scene.** A large borderless charcoal canvas on one display. It starts centered at about 88% of the display width and 86% of its visible height, capped at 1440×900 with a 720×480 minimum. Drag the empty canvas to move it; resize from any edge. Screen changes keep the one Scene clamped to the visible display. Header begins at about 8% from the leading edge and 12% from the top: mute **Focus scene**, the intention in responsive SF Rounded, then a gold tabular count-up. A thin full-width gold horizon arcs through the lower middle; it is ambient geometry, never progress. **End focus** and **Back to window** sit at the lower corners. Back restores the normal window without changing apps or the exact Focus; the normal window exposes the same direct sun-and-horizon entry again. The native horizon breathes almost imperceptibly over 12 seconds; Reduce Motion keeps it static. The Scene owns no new font, raster asset, data, ring, deadline, title bar, pin state, or macOS full-screen Space. A pending Focus Guard decision replaces both exit actions.

**Compact modes.** Classic and Mini have two fixed sizes; only the transient
Focus Scene resizes freely.

- **Classic — 320×460.** The full instrument: caption, aperture, verb, Idle chrome (Today, History, Data, Guard). Fields are `FlowField`: delayed autofocus; a single hairline marks the slot, and it brightens and blooms on focus. No key legend. Intention Return performs the visible Idle action; capture **Park** / **Discard** (Return / Esc still work); Reflection Return completes (text is saved as you type).
- **Mini — 168×176.** Aperture owns the tile. Clock (and gold accrual on Focus) in the hole; verb on the lower arc. Mute and pin sit left; the presentation control sits right. No fields. Typing (Intention, capture, Reflection) expands to Classic. Tap the Mini tile on Idle or Reflection to expand. Prime exposes Focus scene as one compact icon beside Focus now. During Focus the sun-and-horizon primary action enters Scene and its disclosure changes Classic/Mini. `+` in Mini Focus opens capture in Classic; the parked-review icon opens the Reflection chooser in Classic. Focus Scene temporarily replaces either saved mode without overwriting it.

Shared tokens live in `FlowmoLook` (`Atmosphere.canvas`, `Aperture`, `ApertureRing`, `PhaseColumn`, `InstrumentClock`, `Accrual`, `InkButton`, `QuietButton`, `RecoveryVerbs`). Mac fields live in `FlowField`. Menu bar and widget use the same field and ink. Idle glance is mute; Recovery Pause is faint.

Out: theme packs, glass as identity, Focus progress ring, uppercase kickers as a system, serif inside the hole.
