Flowmo · Design Directions

Three philosophies, not three accent swatches. Each prototype runs the full locked loop — Idle → Prime → Focus (count-up, no ring) → Break (earned) → Recall → Close — on both the Mac window and iPhone. Use the phase stepper to jump around; use ×20 to see time move.

01

Desk instrument

Black field. SF. Cyan rationed to moving time and one primary action. Intention is the hero at idle; numerals are the hero at focus.

 02

Warm paper

Off-white page, dark ink, one dried-ink mark. Rest and writing feel like paper; focus feels like a pen on a page, not a cave.

 03

Phase atmosphere

Same compact frame, but material and contrast shift with the loop: dry idle, still prime, near-silent focus, soft break, editorial recall.
